Run the numbers yourself.
With your figures, not ours.

Example for a distributor with €1.2bn revenue, 12% gross margin, stock at 12% of revenue and 400 people in sales. Expected: 1% more revenue from lost requests (€1.4m), 0.3 points margin uplift (€3.6m) and €15m of stock (10.5%) sold earlier at 9% holding cost (€1.4m), together €6.4m a year. Cost in year one: Group license €450,000, implementation €150,000, own effort €32,000. Payback after about 2.4 months, 3-year ROI about 950%. Conservative (0.5% revenue, 0.1 points, 3.5% of stock): €2.4m a year, payback after about 6 months, 3-year ROI about 290%. On top, 10 to 20% of sales time freed, 40 to 80 full-time people, not counted.

How we calculate
  1. Gross profit = revenue × gross margin.
  2. Captured requests = revenue × extra revenue % × gross margin.
  3. Margin uplift = revenue × margin points.
  4. Stock = revenue × stock % × share sold earlier × holding cost. Avoided write-downs are not counted.
  5. Value per year = (captured requests + margin uplift + stock) × share of the business run through DemandMatch.
  6. Freed capacity = people × time freed × cost per person. It only counts if you include it.
  7. Year 1 brings 50% of the value (ramp-up), years 2 and 3 bring 100% each.
  8. Cost in year 1 = license + implementation + own effort (days × €800). Years 2 and 3 = license. The pilot is credited to year 1.
  9. License: Division €180,000 a year (up to €400m revenue in scope, implementation €60,000), Group €450,000 (up to €2bn, implementation €150,000), Global from €600,000 (implementation from €150,000).
  10. 3-year ROI = (3-year value − 3-year cost) ÷ 3-year cost. Payback = the month in which cumulative value exceeds cumulative cost.

What you should know
about DemandMatch.

What is DemandMatch?

DemandMatch is software for B2B distribution. It matches your inventory with your customers' demand automatically: for every request from email, phone or CRM it finds the right material, warehouse and margin and prepares the offer. The other way round, it finds the customers who buy exactly what sits in overstock or arrives inbound. Your sales team reviews and approves.

Who is DemandMatch for?

For distributors and wholesalers of chemicals, plastics and industrial goods, above all for heads of sales, field sales and inside sales teams who have to bring many requests and a lot of stock together.

What does DemandMatch bring to a sales team?

More sales, because no request is left waiting and no customer is forgotten. Better margin, because every offer accounts for freight, storage and your pricing rules, and stock that sits gets sold instead of written off. And less manual work: reading, matching and writing the offer run automatically.

What ROI can a distributor with €1bn revenue expect?

At €1.2bn revenue and 12% gross margin we expect €2.4m (conservative) to €6.4m (expected) a year from more sales, better margin and stock sold earlier. Against the Group license, implementation and own effort of about €630,000 together in year one, that pays back in about 2 to 6 months. Freed sales time comes on top. The calculator on this page lets you adjust every assumption. Only money that actually arrives goes into the ROI; freed time is shown next to it and counted only if you choose to.

How much does DemandMatch cost?

The fit check is free. The value assessment costs €20,000 and the 30-day pilot €60,000. Every paid step is credited to the next one: the value assessment in full to the pilot, the pilot 100% to year one of the license if you sign within 30 days of the review. You never pay twice. The license costs per year €180,000 for one division (up to €400m revenue), €450,000 for the whole group (up to €2bn revenue) and from €600,000 globally, plus a one-time implementation and customisation fee from €60,000 (Division) or from €150,000 (Group, Global). All prices net, excl. VAT. In the US: $22,000, $66,000, $198,000, $495,000 and from $660,000, plus from $66,000 or from $165,000 for implementation, excl. sales tax.

Which systems does DemandMatch work with?

DemandMatch reads from the systems you already run: ERP such as SAP, Oracle, Odoo or DATEV, CRM such as HubSpot, the inbox (Outlook, Gmail) and messengers such as WhatsApp or Slack. Nothing is replaced and there is no system change.

Does DemandMatch send offers to customers by itself?

No. DemandMatch prepares, a person decides. No offer and no email goes out before your sales team has seen and approved it.

What happens to our data?

No system change, no big project. DemandMatch has read-only access to ERP, CRM and inbox and changes nothing there. Your data stays where it is. We set up the connection and access rights together with your IT.

How do we get started?

With a free fit check: you send us a few facts first (industry, revenue, ERP, size of your sales team), so we only talk where it fits. Then a value assessment on one division's data and a 30-day pilot on your real data, for one product family and one team, with success criteria agreed in writing upfront. Week 1: connect read-only. Week 2: match your real requests against your real stock, with your rules on margin and customers. Weeks 3 and 4: your team works with it, and together we measure what it brings.
