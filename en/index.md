HDPE food grade (Not sold yet). Inbound week 49 and Hamburg stock. Special price −€40/t until month end. Released by product management.

- Hanse Closures GmbH: Fits, 24 t. Buys HDPE for closures every 3 weeks. Next call-off in about 9 days. (SAP history)
- Weser Getränketechnik GmbH: Fits, 12 t. Ran HMA 016 in spring. The same grade. (SAP history)
- Sauerland Haushaltswaren GmbH: Maybe, 4 t. Said on the phone that HDPE is worth a trial. (Call)
- Emsland Rohrsysteme GmbH: Left out. Needs PE100 pipe grades. Not a substitute. (SAP)
- Folienwerk Nordheide GmbH: Left out. Film in LLDPE. This grade does not fit. (SAP)

40 t to 3 customers, 2 left out on purpose. Three personal emails wait for approval.

## Questions and answers

### What is DemandMatch?

DemandMatch is software for B2B distribution. It matches your inventory with your customers' demand automatically: for every request from email, phone or CRM it finds the right material, warehouse and margin and prepares the offer. The other way round, it finds the customers who buy exactly what sits in overstock or arrives inbound. Your sales team reviews and approves.

### Who is DemandMatch for?

For distributors and wholesalers of chemicals, plastics and industrial goods, above all for heads of sales, field sales and inside sales teams who have to bring many requests and a lot of stock together.

### What does DemandMatch bring to a sales team?

More sales, because no request is left waiting and no customer is forgotten. Better margin, because every offer accounts for freight, storage and your pricing rules, and stock that sits gets sold instead of written off. And less manual work: reading, matching and writing the offer run automatically.

### Which systems does DemandMatch work with?

DemandMatch reads from the systems you already run: ERP such as SAP, Oracle, Odoo or DATEV, CRM such as HubSpot, the inbox (Outlook, Gmail) and messengers such as WhatsApp or Slack. Nothing is replaced and there is no system change.

### Does DemandMatch send offers to customers by itself?

No. DemandMatch prepares, a person decides. No offer and no email goes out before your sales team has seen and approved it.

### What happens to our data?

DemandMatch has read-only access to ERP, CRM and inbox and changes nothing there. Your data stays where it is. We set up the connection and access rights together with your IT.

### How do we get started?

With a 30-day pilot on your real data. Week 1: connect read-only. Week 2: match your real requests against your real stock, with your rules on margin and customers. Weeks 3 and 4: your team works with it, and together we measure what it brings.
